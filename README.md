# Psych Quest: REBT, Reality & Behavior Therapy

A playable, gamified study reviewer for three counseling theories:
Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy (Albert Ellis), Reality Therapy (William Glasser, Robert Wubbolding),
and Behavior Therapy (B.F. Skinner, Albert Bandura, plus the third-wave approaches).

**Play it:** https://vinzy0.github.io/psych-quest-rebt-reality/

- 9 units, 25 lessons, 46 learn cards, 161 questions, 9 match rounds
- Hearts, XP, streaks, a guidebook per unit, Practice Mistakes, and a Final Boss with every review question
- Single HTML file, no build step. Progress is saved in your browser only.
